# Reference interpretation

The design was informed by a privately supplied NieR screenshot. That third-party image is excluded from this public distribution. The transferable relationships are documented below; the normative palette and identity are fully specified in STYLE.md and the tokens. No game asset or redistribution permission is included.

## Transfer relationships, not screen structure

| Observed aesthetic relationship | Transferable design principle |
| --- | --- |
| Warm pale canvas and dark ink | Neutral surface continuity and clear text contrast |
| Strong title with quiet supporting information | Deliberate typographic and attention hierarchy |
| Precise edges, rows, and quantities | Alignment discipline and clear comparison relationships |
| Flat dark selected region | Bounded inversion with meaningful selection semantics |
| Thin rules and limited ornamental containers | Functional separation and visual restraint |
| Space around the primary subject | Negative space supports the actual content priority |

The screenshot also contains horizontal navigation, an inventory list, a central object view, a status column, and controller controls. Those are task-specific arrangements. They are not reusable page requirements, breakpoint guidance, or evaluation targets for unrelated products.

Departure Mono is the user's identity choice, not a claim about the screenshot's font. The supplied palette is authoritative rather than sampled pixels. Faint game labels, clipped content, and controller glyphs are not accessibility standards or assets to copy.

Alien (1979), Alien: Isolation, cassette futurism, and retro-industrial systems contribute operational clarity, restrained technical identification, and discrete feedback. No secondary film/game imagery is bundled and no asset license is implied. Their darker terminal associations do not replace the primary light identity or permit black/green hacker styling.

## ASCII Studio reference

[ASCII Studio preview](https://motionsites.ai/?prompt=ascii-studio) inspected 2026-10-18 through a real browser. Successive rendered preview frames showed dense character fields changing between architectural forms and text states. This was an embedded preview, not inspection of the underlying interactive site or its implementation. The reusable influence is character-based form and transformation at a meaningful scale. Its black/neon styling, scrambled copy, page structure and assets are not adopted. No remote media is bundled.

## Aesthetic review

Assess whether warmth, hierarchy, alignment, restrained selection, typography roles, meaningful ASCII, and purposeful motion form a coherent identity in the product's own composition. Use [the rubric](../validation/rubric.md). Do not compare a landing page's region count or a mobile task's navigation sequence against an inventory screen. Reference fidelity concerns visual relationships and character, alongside the user's established restrictions.
