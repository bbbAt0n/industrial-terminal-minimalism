# Reference interpretation

The transferable relationships are documented below; identity relationships are specified in STYLE.md; palette derivation belongs to [palette.md](palette.md), with shipped tokens as defaults only.

## Transfer relationships, not screen structure

| Observed aesthetic relationship | Transferable design principle |
| --- | --- |
| Warm pale canvas and dark ink | Neutral surface continuity and clear text contrast |
| Strong title with quiet supporting information | Deliberate typographic and attention hierarchy |
| Precise edges, rows, and quantities | Alignment discipline and clear comparison relationships |
| Flat dark selected region | Bounded inversion with meaningful selection semantics |
| Thin rules and limited ornamental containers | Functional separation and visual restraint |
| Space around the primary subject | Negative space supports the actual content priority |

Navigation, lists, a central object view, status columns and controller controls are task-specific arrangements. They are not reusable page requirements, breakpoint guidance, or evaluation targets for unrelated products.

The technical font is an optional default example, not a mandatory face. The supplied palette is a default example; project/brand colours take precedence. Faint labels, clipped content and controller glyphs are not accessibility standards or assets to copy.

Cassette Terminal and retro-industrial systems contribute operational clarity, restrained technical identification and discrete feedback. Darker terminal associations can inform a project-derived material palette; they do not prescribe black/green hacker styling.

## Product-aware reference families

These are conceptual analysis families, not four packaged themes, a requirement to use every influence, or claims of exact reproduction. Select relationships that serve the discovered product and preserve its chosen identity and accessibility needs.

| Family | Useful product interpretation | Tradeoff to consider |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm Paper Menu | Warm beige continuity, inverted selected row/tab, a large central object with air, quiet status column, hairlines and footer actions for sustained inspection | Transfer the hierarchy, not the inventory layout; urgent changes must remain visible |
| Cassette Terminal | Instrument labels, operational grouping, explicit feedback and deliberate stepped motion for control tasks | Technical character must not turn ordinary language into fake commands |
| Signal Board | Color as signal (acid lime/orange against graphite/mint), heavy grotesk with tiny mono labels, giant unit numbers, spec cards and telemetry bars for real priorities | Abstract scale and signal roles through project tokens; avoid competing accents or cyberpunk neon |
| Research Poster | Mint field, huge black heading, thin guides and mono captions, generous air, dense stats row and one orange accent for explanation and investigation | Preserve the scale/density contrast without burying frequent controls or comparable data |

These illustrative quality references supply principles, never assets to copy or bundle; these colour relationships are valid palette directions when justified by the product. Derive semantic roles and validate them through [palette.md](palette.md); do not copy a proprietary UI. Follow actual product goals, not a mechanical family-to-A/B/C assignment. Apply the [distinctness, boldness and preview contract](../reasoning/composition.md#three-creative-directions) across all three options. Keep established semantic tokens unless a product-specific need warrants a change. Inspect supplied references before claiming details; never copy a recognizable proprietary UI.

## Character transformation reference

Character-based form and transformation can operate at a meaningful scale. Select coherent silhouettes and purposeful transitions without copying external page structure or assets. License and font credits are recorded in [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).

## Aesthetic review

Assess whether material-like palette character, hierarchy, alignment, restrained selection, typography roles, meaningful ASCII, and purposeful motion form a coherent identity in the product's own composition. Use [the rubric](../validation/rubric.md). Do not compare a landing page's region count or a mobile task's navigation sequence against an inventory screen. Reference fidelity concerns visual relationships and character, alongside established project restrictions.

The visual language draws on warm paper system menus, industrial terminals, cassette futurism and technical posters; no third-party artwork, screenshots or assets are included. See [notices](../NOTICE.md).
